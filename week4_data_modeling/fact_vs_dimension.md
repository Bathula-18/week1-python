# Fact Table and Dimension Table

## What is Fact Table?
Fact table stores measurable business values or metrics.

Examples:
- sales_amount
- quantity
- revenue
- profit

Fact table mostly contains numbers.

---

## What is Dimension Table?
Dimension table stores descriptive details used for filtering and grouping.

Examples:
- customer_name
- city
- product_name
- category

---

## Example

Fact Table:
fact_sales

Columns:
- product_id
- customer_id
- quantity
- sales_amount

---

Dimension Tables:

dim_customer
- customer_id
- customer_name
- city

dim_product
- product_id
- product_name
- category

---

## Difference

Fact Table:
- stores metrics
- contains numbers
- used for calculations

Dimension Table:
- stores descriptions
- used for filtering and grouping
